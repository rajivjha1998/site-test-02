Our story

Why we bake

We believe good bread should be part of everyday life, not a special treat. That is why we bake sourdough the patient way and serve it right here in our Leeds neighbourhood.

Who we bake for

Our doors open early for office workers who want a proper breakfast on the way in, and our weekends are made for families who want to linger over brunch. Whether you come in for a single loaf or a celebration cake, you get the same care.

What you will find

  • Sourdough loaves baked fresh
  • Pastries that follow the seasons
  • A relaxed weekend brunch
  • Celebration cakes made to order

Cakes for the moments that matter.

Birthdays, anniversaries, office farewells: a good cake brings people together. We make every celebration cake to order, around your occasion and your taste.
