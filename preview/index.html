What we bake for you.

  • Sourdough loaves

    Slow-risen bread with a crisp crust and an open crumb, baked for your table and your lunchbox.

  • Seasonal pastries

    Pastries that change with the seasons, perfect with a morning coffee on the way to work.

  • Weekend brunch

    A relaxed Saturday and Sunday brunch for families and friends who want good food without the fuss.

  • Custom celebration cakes

    Cakes made to order for birthdays, office parties and family milestones. Book a call to plan yours.
